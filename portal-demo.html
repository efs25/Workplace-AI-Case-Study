About this companion and its evidence boundary

Based on “Designing and Implementing the Prairie Fire Partners Operating System,” final version, including “Expert informed research and feedback.” Source status is as described in the November 10, 2026 artifact, not a live capability check.

All client names, people, project identifiers, conversations and financial records in the interactions are fictional. Public-source briefing facts are separately labeled and linked. Actions change only this page’s temporary state. Reloading clears progress. This file makes no automatic network requests. Source links open external websites only when selected. The offline interactions require no network connection. It contains no client records, private links, credentials or live integrations.

The companion case study is the content reference, including the expert-informed feedback loop and separate evidence-to-destination, contribution-to-compensation and financial-review gates. The paper reports implemented client read controls and a reviewed publication example. It also describes intended contribution, finance, conversational-access and portable-context integrations. This simulation is not a production portal, access-control audit, autonomous learning system or claim of measured business impact.

Design follows the PFP Harbor light reference verified in the current portal workspace stylesheet; this companion does not reproduce every production screen. No special fonts or external assets are required.
